Confidential system overview

Core capabilities

Private business project

A confidential vacancy-management application built for a large FMCG company. New vacancies are encoded and maintained in Power Apps, while the same workspace is used to monitor recruitment progress. Screenshots, company identifiers, internal role names, and business data are intentionally excluded.

01
Vacancy encoding & editing

Users can create new vacancy records in Power Apps and maintain key vacancy information as requirements change.

02
End-to-end vacancy monitoring

The app centralizes vacancy status, recruitment stages, ownership, and progress so records can be monitored in one place.

03
Bulk upload & bulk actions

Multiple vacancies can be uploaded from a structured template, with selected updates handled in bulk to reduce repetitive work.

04
Search, filtering & export

Users can locate records through search and filters, then export vacancy data for reporting or offline review.

05
Approval & hiring-progress tracking

The tracker records approval progress and hiring milestones without exposing the organization’s internal approval structure in this portfolio.

06
Recruitment planning indicators

The system supports sourcing-plan changes, expected hiring timelines, and recruitment lead-time indicators for ongoing vacancies.

●

This portfolio intentionally shows features only. No screenshots, company name, business records, applicant information, or confidential workflow details are displayed.
